# SnapAI Studio

## Register

product

## Users

Independent brands, shop owners, and marketers turning existing product photographs into studio shots and campaign creatives, usually at a desk in daylight.

## Product purpose

Complete the path from product upload to a saved, downloadable image. Make creative direction accessible without pretending AI output guarantees commercial results.

## Brand personality

Considered, precise, approachable. The user asked for a modern, minimal, premium AI SaaS with limited landing layout changes.

## Anti-references

Generic purple gradients, glass cards, fabricated social proof, placeholder features, excessive layout changes, and stock images presented as real AI output.

## Design principles

Keep the current landing narrative. Let photography demonstrate the category. Make costs and next steps clear. Prioritize usable workflows over ornamental UI. Preserve ownership and privacy.

## Accessibility

Keyboard access, visible focus, responsive controls, reduced motion, labeled forms, and inline error states.
