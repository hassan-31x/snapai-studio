# Design

Geist is the single interface typeface. Light canvas and tinted neutral surfaces suit daytime creative work; charcoal primary controls and a small blue accent provide hierarchy. Landing copy uses a 60px desktop display scale with a 36px mobile scale. Product headings use 30px. Controls use 14px or 16px. Main spacing steps are 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80 and 96px. Cards have 12px corners and thin full borders; photography is never wrapped in decorative glass. Product motion conveys state, respects reduced motion, and uses an ease-out curve.
